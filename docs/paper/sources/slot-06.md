## How it supports the paper
This is the official NDRRMC tally for the single worst Metro Manila flood event of the last several years, and it is disaggregated to NCR, so the problem statement can quote a Metro-Manila-specific number rather than a national one. Three-quarters of a million NCR residents affected — with 584,867 of them displaced *outside* formal evacuation centres, i.e. sheltering with relatives or in ad hoc places — establishes both the scale of the hazard and the dispersed, street-by-street nature of the impact that a street-level depth-reporting system like Antas is meant to address. The gap between the 169,579 people captured inside evacuation centres and the 584,867 outside them is itself an argument that official channels see only part of the flooding picture, motivating crowd-sourced ground-level reporting. Because the figure comes from the final sitrep in the series, it reflects completed validation rather than a mid-event estimate.

## Caveats
- **Reachability:** ndrrmc.gov.ph itself blocked every direct request from this machine (HTTP 403 on both the article page and the PDF paths), and monitoring-dashboard.ndrrmc.gov.ph did not connect at all. The document was obtained from the Internet Archive's capture of the canonical NDRRMC URL. The canonical URL is very likely to resolve normally from a Philippine connection or an ordinary browser; if a marker cannot open it, the Wayback link above is a permanent fallback and should be given alongside it.
- **Figures were revised upward substantially over the event.** NCR affected persons went from 125,491 (SitRep 17, 26 July) to 754,446 (SitRep 46, 26 August). Cite the sitrep number and date explicitly — an undated "NDRRMC said X" would be indefensible. Do not mix numbers from different sitreps in the same sentence.
- **NCR vs national:** 754,446 persons is the NCR-only figure and is the one to use for a Metro Manila claim. 6,498,918 persons is the *national* total for the same event and must never be described as a Metro Manila number.
- **Event attribution:** the sitrep covers the Southwest Monsoon *combined* with TC Butchoy and TC Carina from 11 July 2024 onward, not the 24 July flooding alone. Phrase it as "the July 2024 Habagat flooding enhanced by Typhoons Butchoy and Carina" rather than attributing the whole figure to a single day.
- The NCR row is read from a wide table; the layout-extracted row is reproduced verbatim above and the components sum correctly to the stated totals, but anyone re-checking should look at the "AFFECTED POPULATION as of (August 25, 2024 21:00)" regional table in the PDF (NCR section begins around p. 164 of 436).
